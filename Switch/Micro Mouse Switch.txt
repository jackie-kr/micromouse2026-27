ALCO AS2D Switch

This is a 6 pin ON-ON Slide Switch for through-hole PCB mounting

Contact Rating: 50mA at 48V AC/DC

Pin Setup---------------------------------------------------------
The Pin setup looking at the side with text left to right is 1,2,3
Opposite side left to right is 6,5,4

The switch will take input through pin 2 or 5 and then output to the active pin on the corresponding side
The two sides are separate, meaning each side requires its own power input, but both sides can be used at the same time

Switch Position---------------
switch towards 1, pins 1 and 4  
switch towards 3, pins 3 and 6

Switch variations list----------------------------------
https://www.nidec-components.com/e/catalog/switch/as.pdf

-The AS2D switch only has positions for both sides being either on or off
-The AS1D switch specifically has a built in middle position that functions as a fully off mode
